# Discussion Summary 1
**Project:** Digital Privacy Agreement Audit  
**Team Members:** Tirth Laheri, Jay Beladiya

## Objective
The purpose of this discussion was to understand the project requirements, decide how to divide the workload, and identify a strategy for comparing privacy agreements from different organizations.

## Discussion

We began by reviewing the project instructions together and discussing the expected deliverables, including the individual privacy policy analyses, regulatory comparison, summary matrices, and final report. Since the project required analyzing multiple organizations from different industries, we agreed that selecting companies from diverse sectors would provide a more comprehensive comparison of privacy practices.

Before finalizing the companies, **Tirth conducted an initial review of several privacy policies, including Google, HoYoverse (Genshin Impact), FedEx, Discord, Robinhood, Riot Games, USPS, Amazon, PayPal, Discover, Meta, Netflix, Ubisoft, and Microsoft.** After comparing their privacy practices, data collection methods, industry representation, and regulatory coverage, **Tirth selected Robinhood, Riot Games, and USPS for the detailed privacy policy analysis.** These organizations were chosen because they represent three distinct sectors—financial services, gaming, and government services—allowing the project to compare privacy practices across organizations with different legal obligations, business models, and approaches to handling personal information.

Jay analyzed the remaining companies selected for the project to ensure that the overall analysis covered a broad range of industries, including e-commerce, finance, social media, entertainment, gaming, and technology. This distribution provided a balanced comparison of organizations with different privacy practices and regulatory requirements.

During the discussion, we identified an important challenge. Each company structures its privacy policy differently, making direct comparisons difficult. Some organizations provide detailed explanations of user rights, while others organize information by product, service, or geographic region. We realized that comparing the policies without a common evaluation framework could lead to inconsistent findings and make the final report difficult to integrate.

To address this issue, we agreed to create a standardized comparison framework. Every privacy agreement would be evaluated using the same criteria, including data collected, methods of collection, data sharing practices, user rights, security measures, policy updates, opt-out options, and compliance with major privacy regulations such as the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and VCDPA.

## Outcome

- Reviewed the assignment requirements and project deliverables.
- Tirth reviewed multiple privacy policies before selecting Robinhood, Riot Games, and USPS for detailed analysis.
- Jay analyzed the remaining selected organizations to ensure broad industry coverage.
- Identified differences in privacy policy structure as the primary challenge.
- Agreed to use a standardized comparison framework for all organizations.
- Planned a second discussion after completing the individual analyses to combine findings into the final report.
