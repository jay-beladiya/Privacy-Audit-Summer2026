# Discussion Summary 1
**Project:** Digital Privacy Agreement Audit  
**Team Members:** Tirth Laheri, Jay Beladiya

## Objective
The purpose of this discussion was to understand the project requirements, decide how to divide the workload, and identify a strategy for comparing privacy agreements from different organizations.

## Discussion

We began by reviewing the project instructions together and discussing the expected deliverables, including the individual privacy policy analyses, regulatory comparison, summary matrices, and final report. Since the project required analyzing multiple organizations from different industries, we agreed that selecting companies from diverse sectors would allow us to make more meaningful comparisons.

After discussing several options, we divided the work evenly. Jay selected Amazon, PayPal, Discover, Meta, Netflix, Ubisoft, Google, and Microsoft, while Tirth analyzed Robinhood, Riot Games, and USPS. This distribution ensured that we covered financial services, technology, gaming, entertainment, social media, and government organizations.

During the discussion, we identified an important challenge. Each company structures its privacy policy differently, making direct comparisons difficult. Some organizations provide detailed explanations of user rights, while others organize information by product or region. We realized that comparing the policies without a common evaluation framework could lead to inconsistent findings.

To address this issue, we agreed to create a standardized comparison framework. We decided that each privacy agreement would be evaluated using the same criteria, including data collected, data sharing practices, user rights, security measures, policy updates, opt-out options, and regulatory compliance with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and VCDPA.

## Outcome

- Reviewed the assignment requirements.
- Divided the companies between both team members.
- Identified inconsistency in privacy policy structures as the primary challenge.
- Agreed to use a common evaluation framework for all companies.
- Planned a second discussion after completing the individual analyses.
