# Discussion Summary 2
**Project:** Digital Privacy Agreement Audit  
**Team Members:** Tirth Laheri, Jay Beladiya

## Objective
The purpose of this discussion was to review our individual privacy policy analyses, compare our findings across organizations, complete the regulatory comparison, and finalize the structure of the project report.

## Discussion

After completing our individual analyses, we met to compare the privacy practices of the organizations we had selected. Tirth presented the findings for **Robinhood, Riot Games, and USPS**, while Jay shared the analyses for **Google, PayPal, and Ubisoft**. We compared how each organization collects personal information, the types of data they retain, how they share information with third parties, and the privacy rights available to users.

As we reviewed the policies, we observed clear differences between industries. Robinhood and PayPal emphasized financial security, identity verification, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance due to the sensitive financial information they process. Google focused heavily on personalized services, advertising, account integration, and data collected across multiple products. Riot Games and Ubisoft collected gaming-related information such as gameplay activity, account information, communications, and anti-cheat data, while USPS primarily collected information necessary for mail delivery, customer services, and government operations.

We also compared how each organization addressed user privacy rights under major regulations such as the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and VCDPA. Although all six organizations provided mechanisms for users to access, update, or delete personal information where applicable, we found differences in the level of detail provided, the ease of exercising those rights, and the transparency of data-sharing practices.

Another topic of discussion was how to present our findings consistently. Since each privacy policy used different terminology and organizational structures, we agreed to summarize every organization using the same evaluation criteria. This made it easier to create the comparison matrix and identify similarities, differences, strengths, and areas where privacy disclosures could be improved.

Finally, we reviewed the overall report to ensure consistent formatting, remove duplicate information, verify that every company was evaluated using the same framework, and confirm that our conclusions accurately reflected the evidence from the privacy policies.

## Outcome

- Reviewed and discussed the findings from all six selected organizations.
- Compared privacy practices across financial services, technology, gaming, and government sectors.
- Completed the regulatory comparison using GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and VCDPA requirements.
- Finalized the comparison matrix highlighting similarities, differences, strengths, and weaknesses.
- Standardized the formatting and evaluation criteria across all analyses.
- Reviewed the final report for consistency, accuracy, and completeness before submission.
