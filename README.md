# DATA6550 – Digital Privacy Agreement Audit

## Team Members
- **Tirth Laheri**
- **Jay Beladiya**
  
## Project Overview

This project analyzes and compares privacy agreements from organizations across multiple industries. The objective is to evaluate how different organizations collect, use, store, share, and protect user data while assessing their compliance with major privacy regulations.

The analysis includes:
- Individual privacy policy evaluations
- Comparison of privacy practices across industries
- Regulatory compliance assessment
- Summary matrices and comparison tables
- Team discussions documenting the project workflow

## Organizations Analyzed

### Tirth Laheri
- Robinhood
- Riot Games
- United States Postal Service (USPS)

### Jay Beladiya
- Google
- PayPal
- Ubisoft

## Regulations Evaluated

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA)
- Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)

## Project Deliverables

- Privacy policy analyses for six organizations
- Regulatory comparison using GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and VCDPA
- Cross-company comparison matrix
- Team discussion summaries
- Final project report
